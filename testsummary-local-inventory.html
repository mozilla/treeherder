TestSummary Inventory (local file)

Runs the same analysis as scripts/testsummary-inventory.mjs entirely in your browser — but over local files you pick or drop here. Nothing is uploaded; the files are read directly off disk. For each *_testsummary.jsonl artifact it builds an inventory of what survives the TestSummaryFormatter — which record actions, fields, and values are not filtered out — and flags anything still present that the formatter's rules say to drop or strip. Select multiple files to aggregate them into a single inventory.
