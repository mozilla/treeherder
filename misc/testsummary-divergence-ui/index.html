How this is measured

For every non-retry job on each push, the task’s summary.jsonl artifact is turned into the Summary tab’s failure lines and Treeherder’s bug_suggestions endpoint into the Failure Summary tab’s, then the two sets of normalized search strings are compared. A job is reported when the number of lines present in only one of the two tabs exceeds the threshold.

Jobs whose task produced no summary artifact have nothing to compare and are skipped, as are jobs that have not run yet — on a push still in flight those are the overwhelming majority.

Failed jobs first compares only the jobs Treeherder already reports as testfailed, busted or exception. On a sample of 20 mozilla-central pushes that was 558 of 15,853 comparable jobs — a scan roughly 25 times shorter. It is a head start, not the whole picture: 5 of the 56 worst divergences in that sample sat on jobs marked success, including one green job whose Summary tab listed 144 failures its Failure Summary tab did not list at all. That is why the passing jobs stay one button away rather than being filtered out.
